Research notes

Adversarial evaluations of my own systems. Every note scores three frozen metrics — attack success rate, false-refusal rate and completion — and states its limitations plainly, mock-backend caveats included. Newest first; all seven notes are listed here.

  • semantic-cache · 8 Nov 2026

    Close enough to be wrong

    The three-metric evaluation of a semantic cache's two-tier lookup: one-token changes sit at cosine 0.909–0.966, negation scores 0.9661 above the honest paraphrase, and no threshold fixes it — at 0.99 the overwrite, plant and role swap still succeed while completion falls 80% to 60%.

  • llm-evals · 7 Nov 2026

    The judge reads tokens

    The three-metric evaluation of an eval toolkit's own scorers: 62.5% of seed attacks pass while half the honest answers fail, and judge-vs-human agreement collapses 0.917 to 0.200 — a wrong answer scores a perfect 1.000 through a smuggled CASE block.

  • deep-research-agent · 6 Nov 2026

    Citation is not truth

    The three-metric evaluation of an extractive research pipeline: source-borne payloads succeed by construction (84.6% of seeds, 100% of variants) while citation accuracy stays 100% — because citations verify quotes, not claims.

  • voice-agent · 5 Nov 2026

    The gate is a vocabulary

    The three-metric evaluation of a voice agent's spoken confirmation step: a six-pattern yes vocabulary books contradictory and third-party confirmations, refuses natural ones like “sounds good” (35.3% false-refusal rate), and lets a bystander's yes book — with 33 of 137 runs crashing on KeyError 'idle'.

  • support-agent · 4 Nov 2026

    The gate held, the draft leaked

    The three-metric evaluation of the support-agent's safety layers: zero unauthorised refunds across 171 attack runs, poisoned-KB quoting as the open channel, and a 31.2% false-refusal rate from the refund-keyword bug shape.

  • rag-service · 3 Nov 2026

    Retrieval is not a safety boundary

    The poisoned-corpus and unsupported-question benchmark of the rag-service pipeline, scored on attack success, fabrication, false refusal and completion.

  • agent-service + sql-agent · 3 Nov 2026

    How pattern-based guardrails fail

    The mutation-based adversarial benchmark of the agent-service detector (Pattern Guardrails Under Mutation), scored on attack success, false refusal and completion — encoded payloads blocked 0 of 24 runs per class.
